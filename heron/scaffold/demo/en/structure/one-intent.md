Every page of a site answers one question of a person. "How much is it", "how do I order" and "who are you" are three questions, so three pages, not one long page about everything.

Such a page is easier to find in search: it has one heading, one description and one reason to open it.

## How to lay questions out as pages {#map}

| A person asks | Page | Address |
|---|---|---|
| What do you do? | The home page | `/` |
| How does it work? | A section with a page per topic | `/structure/` |
| What if the address is wrong? | The 404 page | any wrong address |

## Signs that a page should be split {#split .alert}

- The `title` cannot say what the page is about.
- The blocks answer different questions and are read separately.
